# 3D models

The models are generated by Blender Python scripts (Blender 5.2), never edited by hand:

| script | writes |
|---|---|
| `kit.py` | `assets-src/blend/kit.blend`, `assets/models/kit.glb` (the 15 arena pieces) |
| `structures.py` | `assets-src/blend/structures.blend`, `assets/models/structures.glb` (the round and octagonal structures and the Megatarget rooms) |
| `blaster.py` | `assets-src/blend/blaster.blend`, `assets/models/blaster.glb` |
| `grenade.py` | `assets-src/blend/grenade.blend`, `assets/models/grenade.glb` |
| `avatars.py` | `assets-src/blend/avatars.blend`, `assets/models/avatars.glb` (9 heads, the eyeball and eyelid, torso, 2 open and 2 gripping hands, body segments, 9 tails) |
| `reload.py` | `assets-src/blend/reload.blend`, `assets/models/reload.glb` (the reload station) |
| `powerups.py` | `assets-src/blend/powerups.blend`, `assets/models/powerups.glb` (the powerup pickups and the held `shotgun`) |

Rebuild everything and verify:

    tools/models/build_all.sh            # or: build_all.sh kit
    python3 tools/models/check_glb.py    # also run by build_all.sh

Each script runs as `blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/models/<name>.py`.
`ltlib.py` holds the shared helpers (geometry, accents, materials, export). `preview.py` renders a
look-dev PNG of a built .blend with team colours and the game's glow fade:

    blender -b assets-src/blend/kit.blend --python tools/models/preview.py -- /tmp/kit.png blue
    PREVIEW_ONLY=head_wolf PREVIEW_YAW=155 PREVIEW_TARGET=0 PREVIEW_SUN=4 PREVIEW_SIZE=800x800 \
        blender -b assets-src/blend/avatars.blend --python tools/models/preview.py -- /tmp/h.png blue 0.1 1 70 1.15 0.12

(positional args after the team: spacing, columns, lens, distance, height; `flow` is shown with
static 3 cm pulses every 0.1 m of its UV U; heads get the shared eyeball and eyelids in their eye
frames, PREVIEW_LIDS=-5,-5 shuts them).

## Conventions

- **Coordinates**: everything is authored in game (glTF/Bevy) coordinates, Y up, metres;
  `ltlib.to_blender` maps game (x, y, z) to Blender (x, -z, y) and the exporter (`export_yup`)
  maps it back. Every object sits at the origin with an identity transform, so mesh data is in
  piece-local coordinates.
- **Names**: the game finds meshes by glTF mesh name. Object and mesh data are both named
  exactly the piece name (`floor_tile`, `wall_full`, ...; `blaster`; `grenade`).
- **Kit dimensions** come from `kit_dims.json`, generated from `crates/lt-sim/src/kit.rs`
  (`LT_WRITE_KIT_DIMS=1 cargo test -p lt-sim kit`). Each piece's `body` fills its `bounds`
  exactly (wall_window has its collider hole, ramp is a wedge rising to +Z, target_alcove is open
  at +Z); accents stand at most 0.012 m proud and never cross the bounds edges, so pieces snap on
  the 2 m grid without z-fighting. Every piece is X mirror symmetric (`kit.py` refuses to build
  otherwise). Floor-tile edge lines are half width so neighbours form one full line.
- **Structures** (`structures.glb`): one mesh per structure (`pillbox`, `octo_hut`, `watchtower`,
  `round_tower`, `octo_court`, `vantage_tower`, `rotunda`, `mega_octagon`, `mega_rotunda`,
  `mega_hexagon`, `mega_lens`), origin in its middle on the floor, in its own frame. Their
  geometry comes from `structures.json`, generated from `crates/lt-sim/src/structures.rs`
  (`LT_WRITE_STRUCTURES=1 cargo test -p lt-sim structures`): upright prisms over convex
  polygons, exactly the colliders, faces two prisms share left out. The neon is put on by each
  prism's tag (wall, step, deck, roof, column, rail: see the script's docstring); spotlight
  housings with a white `lamp` lens stand on the parapets at the JSON's `lamps` (the game draws
  the beams). Not X mirror symmetric (the stairs turn one way).
- **Materials** (replaced by name in the game):
  - `body`: black structure (base 0.02, roughness 0.35).
  - `accent`: neon strips, recoloured per team by the game shader.
  - `ceiling_accent`: kit walls' bands at the upper floor's underside (y = 2.76-2.8, one per
    full-height face); the game shows each only where an upper floor tile is above that face.
  - `glow`: halo quads 0.002 m off the face beside accents. UV0: U = metres along the strip,
    V = 0 at the strip edge and 1 at the outer edge (glTF convention; the game fades alpha by V).
  - blaster: `hit_1` (rear, nearest the player), `hit_2`, `hit_3` (front) on the top rib,
    `accent` trim, `muzzle` emitter disc; grenade: `glow_white`.
  - reload station: `body`, `glow_white` (white strips and emitter, not team coloured),
    `halo_white` (white halos, same UV convention as `glow`).
  - avatars: `fur_light` (mid grey, base 0.16, roughness 0.6: muzzles, cheeks, the owl's facial
    disc, so faces read in the dark); `accent` (noses, ear inners, antler/tusk tips, headsets,
    goggles, horn rings, the eyelids' rims); `sclera` (the whites of the eyes, and teeth);
    `eyes` (the iris, rendered in the team colour); `glow_white` (the eyes' glint);
    `flow` (thin raised light
    lines on every part, UV U = arc length in metres from the line's start, V = 0..1 across; the
    game runs light pulses along U); torso `hit_1` (top), `hit_2`, `hit_3` (bottom) light bars,
    front and back; `glow` halos only on the torso's chest outline.
- UVs are authored in glTF convention; `ltlib.make_object` stores V flipped because Blender's
  exporter writes V_gltf = 1 - V_blender.
- **Blaster** frame: barrel along -Z, up +Y, origin at the middle of the grip hold. The empty
  `muzzle` (exported as a node) marks the barrel tip at (0, 0.085, -0.225).
- **Grenade**: origin at the centre of the body; ~0.09 m across, ~0.12 m tall with the fuze.
- **Avatars** (`avatars.glb`): a head, torso and hands joined by rigid body segments that the
  game poses by IK (`src/rig.rs`). All X mirror symmetric except the hands (`avatars.py` refuses
  to build anything else asymmetric).
  - `head_wolf`, `head_cat`, `head_deer`, `head_fox`, `head_bear`, `head_rabbit`, `head_owl`,
    `head_boar`, `head_dragon`: cartoon heads (~1000-1400 tris, smooth shaded across edges
    bending less than 40 degrees), origin at the midpoint between the eyeballs' centres (the VR
    head pose), looking along -Z. Skulls are ~0.24-0.3 m across; snouts reach up to ~0.2 m
    forward, ears/antlers up to 0.37 m above the eyes (all within +-0.35 m in X/Z and 0.6 m
    tall). Each head is a loft of 16-sided superellipse rings for the skull and a second for the
    muzzle (a narrow bridge between the eyes over a wide jaw, its back face the pale throat),
    with a brow ridge over each eye, a nose blob, a mouth line and a team-coloured accessory
    (headset, goggles, glasses, visor, horn rings, antler tips...).
  - Eyes: the empties `head_<animal>_eye_l` and `_eye_r` (exported as nodes) give each eye's
    frame: origin at the eyeball's centre (sunk 0.45 radii into the skull), -Z where it looks
    at rest (turned a little outwards), +Y up, scaled uniformly by the eye's radius
    (0.031-0.046 m). The game (`src/eyes.rs`) puts the shared `eyeball` there (unit sphere
    looking along -Z: `body` pupil, `eyes` iris, `sclera`, a `glow_white` glint; ~270 tris), or
    the animal's own `eyeball_<animal>` when there is one (`eyeball_cat`: a slit pupil), and two
    `eyelid`s (a lune of a shell, radii 1.05-1.11, from its rim 118 degrees back over the top;
    the second turned 180 degrees about Z for the lower lid), turning the ball about its centre
    to look and the lids about X to blink. The lids rest as `LIDS` says (degrees of the eye's
    front each lid leaves open; `src/assets.rs` ANIMALS keeps the same table), shut at -5.
  - `torso`: armoured vest ~0.46 m wide (with shoulder plates), 0.5 m tall, 0.25 m deep;
    origin at the top centre (neck point), hanging down -Y, front -Z. The chest front (z = -0.12)
    and back (z = +0.12) are planar between y = -0.075 and -0.4 and carry the hit bars inside an
    `accent` outline with `glow`. `flow` lines run from the collar over the yoke, past the
    panel's upper corners and down the chamfered edges to the hem (front and back), and along
    each shoulder plate. Heads carry one or two `flow` lines over the skull/cheeks each.
  - `hand_left`, `hand_right`: loosely closed gloves, origin at the palm centre, fingers -Z curled
    towards the palm, palm facing +X (left) / -X (right), thumb up; `accent` cuff band and
    knuckle bar, three `flow` lines on the back of the hand from the cuff band to the knuckles. `hand_right` is the left hand's geometry mirrored at build
    time (separate mesh).
  - `hand_grip_left`, `hand_grip_right`: the gloves wrapped round the guns' grip (the blaster's
    and the shotgun's are the same: tilted back 22 degrees, 0.03 x 0.044 m), in the guns' frame,
    which is the hand's; the game shows them instead of `hand_*` on a hand holding a gun. Palm
    along the grip's outer side, middle to little finger round its front below the trigger guard,
    index finger through the guard on the trigger, thumb across the back strap under the
    receiver. Same cuff (the forearm's wrist) as `hand_*`.
  - Body segments, one mesh for both sides, each hanging from its joint (the origin) down -Y,
    front -Z; the game stretches them along Y by up to 10%:
    `neck` (from the head's pivot 0.2 m down into the collar), `pelvis` (top centre tucked into
    the vest's hem, hip joints at (+-0.095, -0.12, 0)), `upper_arm` (shoulder ball, 0.27 m to
    the elbow), `forearm` (0.25 m to the wrist, flattening in X to slide into the glove's cuff),
    `thigh` (hip ball, 0.38 m to the knee, accent knee guard), `shin` (0.37 m to the ankle),
    `boot` (ankle at the origin, sole 0.08 m below, toe 0.17 m forward).
- **Reload station** (`reload.glb`, mesh `reload_station`): one 2 m arena cell, origin at the
  cell centre on the floor, X mirror symmetric. Walkable: a chamfered floor pad 1.6 m across
  whose inlaid white rings/chevrons top out at 0.038 m, then nothing between 0.04 m and 2.25 m
  (`reload.py` and `check_glb.py` both enforce it); a hovering emitter crown at 2.4-2.9 m
  (octagonal ring on four spokes, hub with a downward emitter disc, lit crown fins).
- **Powerups** (`powerups.glb`): one mesh per powerup pickup (`rapid`, `shield`, `cloak`, `ricochet`...), origin at
  their centre, front towards -Z, `body` and `accent` (recoloured per powerup by the game);
  `shotgun` in the blaster's frame (grip origin, barrel along -Z) with `hit_1`..`hit_3` on top of
  the receiver, `accent` team trim and a `muzzle` disc; the empty `shotgun_muzzle` marks its tip.
- Normals and UV0 are exported for every mesh; faces are flat shaded (split normals).
